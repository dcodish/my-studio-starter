# Prompts for each round (copy, then edit)

Each round ends with something that works. Leaving early? You leave with what you have.

## Round 0: Open the project (19:15)
Claude app → Code tab → pick this folder. Permission mode: Manual.

    What's in this folder? Explain it to me in 3 short sentences.

Watch what it does before it answers: it reads the files first.
**Habit 1: Claude only knows what is in the folder and the chat. Give context in files.**

## Round 1: Requirements and plan (19:25)
Fill in IDEA.md (5 minutes). Then switch to **Plan** mode and paste:

    Read IDEA.md and CLAUDE.md. Ask me up to 3 questions if something is unclear.
    Then plan the smallest version that does the 3 must-haves. Show me the plan
    in plain words before writing any code.

Answer its questions and fix the plan. Don't approve yet.
**Habit 2: plan before you build.**

## Round 2: Version 1 (19:35)
Approve the plan. While it builds (1–3 minutes), watch the screen at the front.
Then open index.html and click every button. Write down one thing that is wrong
or doesn't look good.
**Habit 3: "I did it" is not "it works". You are the tester.**

## Round 3: Find something broken, fix it (19:50)
Describe what you SEE, not how to code it:

    On my phone the Spin button is hidden under the wheel. Make it visible.
    When I click Start nothing happens. Find out why and fix it.

You can also drag a screenshot into the chat and say what's wrong.
Going the wrong way? Press **Esc** to stop it. Then open the page again and check.

## Round 4: Memory (20:05)
1. `Add to CLAUDE.md: every button must be big enough to tap on a phone.`
2. `/memory`: open the file and see the new line.
3. `/context`: how full is the desk? Then `/clear`.
4. In the clean chat, ask for a new button. Does the rule still hold?

**Habits 4 and 5: an important rule goes in CLAUDE.md. New task = `/clear`.**

## Round 5: The wow moment (20:15)

    Add the wow moment from IDEA.md.

One improvement per request (color, animation, sound, text). Make the browser
window narrow like a phone and check. Works? `/clear`, then the next one.

## Round 6: Publish (20:30)

    Publish index.html as an artifact so I can share it.

Open the page, click **Share**, choose the public link. Open the link on your
phone and check it (habit 3 again). Then send it to the WhatsApp group.

Staying for the open work hour (21:00–22:00)? Keep building, or pick a mission from BONUS.md.
