# Idea menu
Pick one, change it to fit you, or bring your own idea.
Every idea here can be built in about 30 minutes as one web page, and shared as a link.
Difficulty: ★ easy · ★★ a bit more
**Can't decide in 60 seconds? Take #5, the quiz.** It's easy, personal and fun to send.

## Create something to post
1. **Video Studio ★★**: a 6–10 second animated clip for a story, a birthday or a club,
   with your text, recorded straight from the page as a video file (MP4).
2. **Invite / poster maker ★**: type the event details, pick a style, get an original
   poster to download as an image (PNG) and send to the group.
3. **Semester Wrapped ★★**: type a few numbers about your semester (coffees, exams,
   all-nighters...) and get an animated "Wrapped"-style story card to share.
4. **Sticker / meme maker ★**: pick your own photo, add text and stickers, download it.

## Play with friends
5. **"How well do you know me?" quiz ★**: 8 questions about you; friends get a score
   and a funny title at the end.
6. **Decision wheel ★**: a spinning wheel for "where do we eat / who pays / what to watch",
   with sounds and confetti.
7. **Mini arcade game ★★**: Snake, Flappy-style or catch-the-falling-things, with your
   own theme and a high score.

## Make life easier
8. **Exam countdown + focus timer ★**: countdowns to your exams and a Pomodoro timer
   with a motivating vibe.
9. **Trip / apartment expense splitter ★★**: who paid what, and who owes whom, in the
   fewest transfers.
10. **Link-in-bio page ★**: your personal page with links, an animated background and
    a short "about me".

## Want Claude to use your own data?
Connect a service first: Claude app, Settings → Connectors (Google Calendar, Drive...).
Then Claude can read it while it builds, e.g. your exam dates for #8 or photos from a Drive
folder for #2 and #4. Say so in "In my words". The page you publish does NOT get access to
your Calendar or Drive: Claude copies what it needs into the page.

## Your own idea?
Great. Keep it to one page, with no login and no database, and something you can show in 30 minutes.
